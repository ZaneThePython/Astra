(this file won't be here on release) to explain the face.json, eyeSize is the difference between the default eye and the new eye. For example, if it was eyeSize 50 then the eye in this theme will be rendered at half the size of the normal Jibo eye. If it was eyeSize 300 then it would be 3x the size of the normal Jibo eye. If it was eyeSize 100 then it would be the same size as the normal eye. There will also be support for background.png in the face folder which will replace the background with a 1280x720 image.

THere is also eye.png and eyeClosed.png. Basically, eye.png is what his regular eye will be, but, eyeClosed.png is an image in the same exact format except it's what it will be when it's closed. The more that his eye is squished, the more that the closed texture will fade in and the regular one will fade out. Now it isn't different from the standard eye.png, it is still a 1:1 ratio, it's just that this one is what he will used when his eye is closed. It will be setup the exact same way as it is when his eye is open in the sense that the closed image will also be squished, it's just that this one will only show as his eye is being squished into a closed position where it will stay as the closed eye, meanwhile the normal texture is what shows when his eye is fully open and the closed texture fades into the open texture as he opens his eye. Being pet, sleeping, blinking, etc.
